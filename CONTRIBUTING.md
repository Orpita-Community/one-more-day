# Contributing to One More Day

Thank you for contributing to "One More Day" and helping us build projects together.

Before submitting any contribution, please read the project's LICENSE and this document.

1. You have the legal right to submit the contribution.

2. The contribution is your original work, or you have obtained the necessary rights and permissions to submit it for use in the Project.

3. You agree that your contribution will be subject to the terms of the project's LICENSE and this CONTRIBUTING.md.

4. You grant the Orpita Game Community a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use, reproduce, modify, adapt, maintain, develop, incorporate, and distribute your contribution as part of the Project and its future versions, including releases made after you leave the Community.

5. You acknowledge that the Community may continue to maintain, modify, develop, and distribute the Project without requiring your ongoing participation or individual approval, subject to the rights granted above.

6. You retain ownership of your original contribution unless otherwise agreed in writing. The license granted to the Community does not by itself transfer ownership of your copyright.

If you do not agree to these terms, please do not submit contributions to the Project.

## Credit and Attribution

Contributors may be credited in CONTRIBUTORS.md and in official project releases where appropriate.

You retain the right to showcase your own contributions as permitted by Section 3 of the project's LICENSE.

Please represent your role and contribution accurately when publishing portfolio or professional material about the Project.

---

## Contribution Guidelines

- Submit only original work or materials for which you have the necessary rights and permissions.
- Do not submit copyrighted, trademarked, licensed, or third-party materials unless their use in the Project is legally permitted and properly documented.
- Do not knowingly submit materials that infringe the rights of another person or organization.
- The Community Representative and designated maintainers may review, accept, reject, modify, or request changes to contributions.
- Contributions should follow the structure, technical requirements, and workflow established for the Project.

---

## External Contributors

Individuals who contribute during a specific event or activity without being members of Orpita Game Community may be recognized as External Contributors.

External Contributors do not automatically receive Team Member status or the showcase permissions described in Section 3 of the LICENSE unless the Community explicitly grants those permissions in writing.

Any contribution from an External Contributor remains subject to the rights and permissions agreed upon at the time of contribution.

---

## Leaving the Community

Leaving Orpita Game Community does not terminate the rights previously granted to the Community for contributions submitted under this document.

Former contributors may continue to showcase their own contributions as permitted by the project's LICENSE.

Further development of the Project by former Team Members should be coordinated through the Official Repository and the Community Representative.
